Game plan

  • Rounds 1 to 4: take the best RB or WR available. Bowers and McBride are the only TEs worth a round 2 pick.
  • QB can wait. Allen is the lone round 3 QB. Burrow, Hurts, Caleb Williams and Herbert go rounds 6 to 8 and score close to the same.
  • Rounds 5 to 9 win PPR leagues. Target-heavy WRs (McConkey, Higgins, McLaurin, Odunze) and pass-catching RBs (Skattebo, Gainwell, Rachaad White) outscore early-down bruisers here.
  • Kicker and defense in rounds 14 and 15. Aubrey and the Texans are the only ones worth an earlier reach.
  • Rookies (Love, Price, Tuten, Tate, Lemon) carry more week-to-week swing. Pair them with a steady vet.
